Company Story

Entertainment with operational care.

SI EGYPTE creates trackless train, kids electric car, and custom entertainment experiences for shopping centres, events, carnivals, and family-focused venues. Our approach blends bright presentation with clear planning so each activation feels enjoyable for guests and manageable for partners.

Based in Egypt and serving communities nationwide, the company supports experiences that can scale from small private events to larger centre activations.
